# E-Commerce Business Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a 100-row e-commerce dataset using Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, products, regions, payment methods, order status, and shipping performance.

The project includes data analysis, PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI development, an interactive dashboard, slicers, and business insights.

## Key Business Insights

- Electronics is the highest-sales category in the dataset.
- North region records the highest sales among the four regions.
- Laptop is the highest-selling product by sales value among the Top 5 products.
- Corporate customers generate the highest sales among the customer segments shown.
- Total sales are approximately ₹1.22M and total profit is approximately ₹289K.
